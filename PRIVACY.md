# GasSnifferBuddy privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 (survey map added)

GasSnifferBuddy is the phone app for a home-built gas detector. It is published
on the App Store and Google Play by Rebel Heat.

**In short: the app does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything
it records stays on your phone unless you choose to share it. The only thing it
downloads is satellite imagery for the survey map.**

## What the app uses, and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bluetooth** | To connect to your gas sensor board and receive its readings. | Nowhere. Readings stay on your phone. |
| **Your location** | To tag each reading with where it was taken, so a survey can be put on a map. It is only used while the app is open. | Saved in your survey files on your phone. Nowhere else. |
| **Your local Wi-Fi network** (optional, off by default) | Only if you turn on "USB bridge via PC" in Settings, to receive readings from a sensor plugged into your own computer. | Between your phone and your own computer. Nothing goes to the internet. |
| **The internet, for map images** | The Surveys screen shows your route over satellite imagery. The app downloads the picture tiles for the area on screen from Esri's World Imagery service. | Esri receives the standard details of any web request: your IP address and which map squares were asked for, which shows roughly where you are looking. No readings, survey files or account details are sent. Esri's privacy policy is at https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview |
| **Storage on your phone** | To save your surveys as CSV files, and to remember your settings and sensor calibration. | Stays on your phone. |

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet for anything except the map images above.
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It has no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
- It does not share data with Rebel Heat or anyone else.

## Sharing your surveys

You can choose to share a survey file, for example by email or to a cloud
drive, using your phone's normal share menu. A survey file contains the gas
readings with their times and GPS positions. Once you share it, the app or
service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any survey from the Surveys screen in the app. Uninstalling the
app deletes all of its surveys, settings and calibration from your phone.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.
